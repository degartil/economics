# Who am I?

My name is Diego Janson, better known as Degartil. Born and raised in the state of Bahia, northeastern Brazil. One day to leave this godforsaken land.

I am by no means an economist, nor am I a writer, and I struggle to call myself even an engineer. But of the three, engineer is the one I am closest to.

However, I am really interested in economics. I hope a few thousand hours in Victoria are enough proof XD

If they are not, my only hope is to fill this repo with enough relevant content over time.

Through economics, I hope to find my way through life and gain a better understanding of the world I live in and the rules that govern it.

# What will you find here?

Various texts on economics, all written in English. Don't expect to find anything academic, though.

I am often assigned to read books and write a "critical review" of them. Not all of those will end up here, but the ones I feel actually have something to say probably will.

There will also be exploratory notes on topics that I think are important, interesting, or where I feel I may have something to contribute. I would not expect novel economic theory from those, though.

If by any chance I produce anything truly interesting, it will be here as well.

The structure is pretty simple, each directory is named after an author whose work the text is related to. Anything based on Mises, Hayek, Marx, etc. will go into the folder named after them.

Ideas that are somewhat original, personal notes, and anything else that is primarily my own will go under `degartil`.

# On AI

None of the texts in this repo are written by AI. If I am going to produce slop, it is better to produce nothing at all, or at least produce artisanal slop.

However, I do use AI extensively in a few ways:

* To do research
* To check whether my arguments make sense
* To help me edit and structure texts
* To help me improve the quality of my writing

There is also the fact that I am not a native English speaker, and AI helps me a lot with grammar, wording, and making the text read more naturally.

Even with AI, I put the utmost effort into every text. If it is a bad text, it is my fault, and I don't like machines taking credit for MY garbage!
